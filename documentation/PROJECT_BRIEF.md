# ProfitTrace | Project Brief

## Business Scenario

An e-commerce business is growing transaction volume, but leadership is concerned that revenue growth is not translating into equivalent profit growth. The analytics team is asked to identify the commercial and operational sources of profit leakage.

## Primary Objective

Build a decision-support solution that traces the path from gross sales to realized profit and identifies where margin is being lost through discounts, refunds, product cost, shipping and return-related costs.

## Analytical Workstreams

### Commercial Profitability
- Revenue and margin by category, subcategory and product
- High-revenue / low-margin opportunities
- Discount intensity and margin trade-offs

### Operational Leakage
- Return rate and refund value
- Return reasons
- Delivery performance
- Late-delivery versus on-time return behavior
- Carrier and shipping-method economics

### Customer Economics
- One-time versus repeat customer value
- Customer profitability
- Acquisition-channel economics
- Segment and regional performance

## Tool Responsibilities

**Excel:** business-friendly operational source files.

**SQL Server / SSMS:** staging, data-quality validation, cleaning, analytical views, business analysis and reconciliation QA.

**Power BI:** dimensional modeling, DAX measures, interactive visuals, cross-filtering and executive storytelling.

## Expected Management Questions

1. Where is profit being created?
2. Where is revenue failing to convert into profit?
3. Which products should receive pricing or promotion review?
4. Which return reasons create the largest financial leakage?
5. Does late delivery coincide with higher return activity?
6. Which customer and acquisition groups deserve more commercial attention?

## Scope Boundary

The dataset is synthetic and behaviorally structured for portfolio analysis. Relationships observed in the data should be described as associations. The project does not claim causal effects from observational records.
